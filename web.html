◈ CITY OF SECRETS
● PRIVATE ROLES · PUBLIC SCANDAL
Case briefing · How to play

3–6 players: hidden-role mystery. 2 players: cooperative detective case. One device per player. Create a room and share its code. Roles are assigned privately by the server.

  1. Explore (50 seconds): click a City Hall room, choose an action, and lock it. Everyone acts simultaneously. Missing players wait in Council. Finance reveals transfer location and shared badge credentials; Security shows visits; Records holds secrets or possibly forged memos; Council offers witness interviews. You also recognize people visiting your room.
  2. The Embezzler: steal $10,000 from Finance or Records, plant an unsigned memo in Records, or scrub your camera entry in Security. Forging and concealing earn no money. You may also search. Steal at least $30,000 and survive six rounds, or cause a wrongful arrest, to win.
  3. Everyone has an embarrassing secret. Cover personal secret removes your expense file for the round but leaves your visit visible. These expenses are not the main theft. Shared badges and footage require corroboration. No ordinary clue states a role.
  4. Discuss (60 seconds): use chat or talk aloud. Your notebook is private; share a clue with its Share button, or type anything—including lies. Only the Investigator can nominate a suspect. All players choosing Ready skips to the next round.
  5. Arrest vote (35 seconds): a strict majority of all humans must approve. Abstentions and ties reject the warrant and advance. Correct arrest: city wins. Wrong arrest: Embezzler wins immediately. At round six, no arrest and at least $30,000 stolen: culprit escapes. Below $30,000, the city protects its treasury and wins. All roles and actual moves are revealed at game end.
  6. Two-player case: both humans are honest: one Investigator and one Official. Three fictional suspects make server-controlled moves; one is stealing. Investigate their visits and files together. Both humans must approve an arrest.

Six rounds take approximately 10–12 minutes plus arrest votes. Timers advance when a connected device polls. Refresh restores your seat in the same tab; use the same browser to recover a closed tab. Rooms expire after 24 hours without activity. Updates use one-second server polling. All scandals are fictional.
